What it does

Turn, tilt and lean

All six degrees of movement, from the Face ID camera on iPhone or our own tracking model on Android. Smoothed on the phone and sent at 60 updates a second by default, or up to 120.

Streams to OBS, fast

Video and noise-cancelled audio go straight into OBS through QuickFeed, our own plugin. A green screen effect without the green screen, background blur and face lighting come with the optional Pro upgrade.

Make the movement your own

Headcam maps your head to the camera 1:1 by default for small leans, glances and peeks. Turn up the sensitivity on any axis for a bigger movement in game, or use OpenTrack for custom curves.

Games that never had it

Free mods on GitHub and Nexus add head tracking to dozens of games, with more on the way. Easy in, easy out. Lopari launches any of them in one click.

Nothing leaves your network

No servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads. Your face stays on the phone. The only things sent to your PC are six numbers describing where your head is, and your video if you choose to stream.

Getting set up

Phone, connection, game. Add OBS whenever you're ready.

1

Mount the phone

Clip it to the monitor or prop it on the desk, front camera facing you.

2

Connect

Same WiFi as your PC. On iPhone you can plug in over USB with the companion app instead.

3

Connect your game

For games with built-in head tracking, use OpenTrack on your PC to receive Headcam's tracking data. You can also use Lopari to launch supported games with tracking on.

4

Stream, if you want it

Install the QuickFeed plugin and your phone shows up in OBS as a camera. Green screen effect, background blur and face lighting come with the optional Pro upgrade.
